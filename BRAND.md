# DF Together brand guide

DF Together is an independent community planning companion. Its visual system is
inspired by the optimistic, clear feeling of the Dreamforce experience, but it
must remain visibly distinct from Salesforce and its products.

## Visual direction

- Bright sky blue (`#0176D3`) is the primary action and brand color.
- Deep navy (`#032D60`) carries headlines and high-contrast text.
- Pale sky (`#DFF3FE`) and cloud (`#F3F9FF`) create the airy background system.
- Fresh green (`#45C65A`) is a small connection, success, and availability accent.
- White surfaces, soft blue borders, continuous corners, and restrained shadows
  keep dense agenda information approachable.
- Orbit and connection motifs represent friends finding one another. They should
  stay abstract and must not form a cloud logo.

## Identity boundaries

- Use the original DF Together mark from [`assets/brand-icon.svg`](./assets/brand-icon.svg).
- Do not use or redraw the Salesforce cloud, Salesforce wordmark, stylized “f”,
  product icons, Trailhead characters, or other proprietary Salesforce artwork.
- Do not put “DF Together” inside a Salesforce-like cloud or create a mark that
  could imply the app is an official Salesforce product.
- Keep the independence and planning-only disclaimer visible on public pages.

## Product use

Blue identifies links, selected navigation, and primary actions. Green communicates
successful connections and explicitly enabled sharing. Orange and red are reserved
for warnings, schedule conflicts, and destructive actions. Never use color as the
only indicator of state.
