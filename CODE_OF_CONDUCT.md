# Community Code of Conduct

DF Together welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or sharing someone else’s private information. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior makes the community unsafe or unproductive.

To report a concern privately, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue.
