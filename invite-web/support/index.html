SUPPORT

How can we help?

DF Together is an open community project. Bug reports and thoughtful contributions are welcome.

Does adding a session reserve my seat?

No. DF Together is a planning companion. It never updates your official Dreamforce registration or guarantees admission.

Why can’t my friend see my agenda?

You must both be accepted friends, and the agenda owner must enable sharing from Profile. Sharing starts off.

What if my agenda has not synchronized?

Your changes remain saved on your device. Reconnect to the internet, open Agenda or Profile, and tap Retry on the sync notice.

Report a problem

Open a GitHub issue with what you expected, what happened, your iPhone model, and the app version. Do not include magic links, invitation codes, or private account information.

Contribute

Developers can review the public repository and contribution guide.
