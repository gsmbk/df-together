PRIVACY

Your agenda belongs to you.

This notice explains the information DF Together uses to provide agenda planning and optional social features.

What we collect

You can browse the bundled Dreamforce catalog and build a device-local agenda without an account. If you sign in, we store your email-based account identifier, display name, selected sessions, friend relationships, invitation records, and your agenda-sharing preference.

How we use it

We use this information only to authenticate you, synchronize your agenda, connect accepted friends, and show shared agendas when the owner has explicitly enabled sharing. We do not sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers.

Agenda sharing

Sharing is off by default. When enabled, only accepted friends can view your selected sessions. Turning sharing off prevents friends from loading your agenda again.

Service providers

DF Together uses Supabase for authentication and synchronized app data, Vercel for this website, Expo for app builds and distribution, and may use Resend to deliver transactional sign-in and invitation email. Those providers process data to deliver their services under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Account data remains while your account is active or as needed to operate and secure the service. To request account or data deletion, contact us through the support page. Device-local agenda data can also be removed by deleting the app.

Independent project

DF Together is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Salesforce. It does not access, reserve, or modify your official Dreamforce agenda.
